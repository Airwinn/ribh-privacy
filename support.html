Common topics

  • Getting started: you can log what you buy and what you sell without internet, and Ribh shows your real monthly profit. Sign-in, sync, AI features and purchases need internet.
  • Subscriptions (Pro / Expert): manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall to recover a plan on a new device.
  • Your data: your records are saved on your phone. When you sign in with an account, the app also backs them up to our servers on every plan. There is no switch to turn this off. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Delete your account & data: sign in, then Settings → Account → Delete account inside the app, or email us to ask for removal. Some records described in the Privacy Policy are not deleted.
